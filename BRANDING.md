# JARVIS branding

The app now has a custom cyan HUD-style launcher icon inspired by the supplied reference: concentric radar rings, a central J mark, cyan glow and small status indicators.

`JarvisOrbView` is a lightweight custom Android canvas view for the live in-app HUD. It rotates its outer arc and indicators while visible, and stops its animation when detached to avoid unnecessary battery use.

The launcher assets are in `app/src/main/res/drawable` and `app/src/main/res/mipmap-anydpi-v26`.
